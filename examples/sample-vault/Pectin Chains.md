# Pectin Chains

Pectin consists of a complex set of polysaccharides present in primary cell walls.

Key characteristics:
- High galacturonic acid content
- Methyl esterification determines gelling properties
- Broken down by pectinase enzymes during ripening and [[Fermentation Chemistry]].
