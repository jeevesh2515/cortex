# Pectin Chains

Pectin consists of a complex structural set of polysaccharides present in primary cell walls and intercellular regions of plants.

Key characteristics:
- High galacturonic acid backbone with varying degrees of methyl esterification.
- Degree of esterification dictates gel formation speed and calcium-binding capacity.
- Broken down by pectinase enzymes during fruit ripening and botanical [[Fermentation Chemistry]].
