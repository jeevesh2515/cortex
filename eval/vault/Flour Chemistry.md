# Flour Chemistry

Wheat flour composition dictates the structural network of baked bread.

Key components:
- Glutenin and gliadin proteins combine with water to form the viscoelastic gluten matrix.
- High protein flour (12-14%) is required for artisan sourdough to retain trapped fermentation gases.
- Alpha-amylase enzymes break down damaged starch into maltose sugars for yeast consumption.
