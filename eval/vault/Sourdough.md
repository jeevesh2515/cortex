# Sourdough Baking

Sourdough bread relies on wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria symbiotic cultures rather than commercial baker's yeast.

Core steps:
- Feeding the starter twice daily with equal parts water and whole grain flour.
- Bulk fermentation: letting the dough rise at room temperature while building gluten strength through stretch-and-fold techniques.
- Final proofing in a banneton basket before baking in a preheated Dutch oven.
