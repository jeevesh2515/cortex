# Fermentation Chemistry

Fermentation kinetics depend heavily on substrate availability and polysaccharide breakdown.

During fruit-based and botanical fermentation, the structural integrity of plant cell walls is mediated by [[Pectin Chains]].
Metabolic pathways convert simple carbohydrates into ethanol, lactic acid, and acetic acid, lowering pH and preserving the medium.
